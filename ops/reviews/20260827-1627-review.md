distinct and reproducible, the taxonomy is closed on both sides of the

because a gate row overstated its done-when. A branch that fixes the scoreboard

No code change is required. The remedy is four edits to `ops/DRIVE-LOG.md`
(the review paragraph, the `npm test` tail and count, the gate-state sentence,
